Overseer Lexicon

Words for the person who runs the place: stewards, foremen, workshop heads, gallery curators, harbour masters and village headmen, from ancient and modern languages.

    About this list

    Era is approximate. Ancient means before about 500 CE, Historic runs to about 1900, and Modern covers terms in current use. Meanings are simplified to the sense of managing, keeping or overseeing something.

    The list was compiled from general knowledge. Spellings, romanizations and glosses may be imperfect, so check them before you cite one.
